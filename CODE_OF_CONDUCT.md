# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and accept
constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community.
Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the
repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability** is also accepted for
conduct reports) or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant apply.
